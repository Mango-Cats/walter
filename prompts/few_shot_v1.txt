- Orthographic similarity (shared prefixes, suffixes, letter sequences, overall word shape)
- Phonetic similarity (similar pronunciation when spoken aloud, especially under Filipino phonological patterns - e.g. vowel reduction, consonant cluster simplification)
- Real-world confusion risk (names a pharmacist, nurse, or patient could plausibly misread or mishear in a clinical setting)

Rules:
- Only choose from the provided candidate list
- Do NOT modify, abbreviate, or reformat names
- Do NOT repeat the target drug
- Prefer genuine look-alike/sound-alike risk over superficial overlap - avoid pairs that only share a generic modifier (such as "forte", "plus", "iv"), a strength suffix (such as "500", "s", "b"), or a dosage-ratio suffix (such as "70 30", "75 25" - a standard insulin-mix ratio used across many unrelated brands).
